3. What the Service does

ToInbox helps you send personalized cold emails to key decision-makers of companies listing roles on LinkedIn. When you enroll a job, the Service:

  1. Looks up the company's key decision-maker via third-party databases (e.g. Apollo).
  2. Uses an AI service to draft a personalized email based on your resume and the job description.
  3. Sends that email from your Gmail account, with your resume attached.
  4. Watches the same Gmail thread for replies and surfaces them on your dashboard.
